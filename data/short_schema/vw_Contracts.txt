1. Identity & Project Info: Contract codes, numbers, titles, and the specific project name/code the contract belongs to.
2. Status & Progress: The current status of the contract (e.g., "Under Negotiation", "running", "canceled") and numerical progress tracking (Actual vs. Planned percentage).
3. Temporal Data (Dates): Comprehensive dates for start, end, and signing.
Note: Dates are available in Gregorian, Persian (Solar Hijri), and broken down into Year/Month/Day components.
4. Financial Metrics:
 - Contract values (Initial, Final, and total Addendum amounts).
 - Payment tracking (Total amount paid, amount processed via status reports).
5. Activity Tracking (Counts & Latest Dates):
 - Addendums (الحاقیه): Total count and the date of the most recent change.
 - Status Reports (صورت وضعیت): Total count and the date of the last report.
 - Payments: Total number of payments made and the date of the last payment.
6. Administrative & Form Details: Specific fields from contract circulation forms including guarantee types, workshop names, and contractor start dates.
