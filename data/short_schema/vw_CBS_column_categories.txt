1. Identity & Label: Unique CBS record identifier and its human-readable name.
2. Organization & Ownership: Which organizational unit this CBS item belongs to, including unit name and any parent stakeholder linkage.
3. Audit Trail (Users): Who created and last modified the record.
4. Audit Trail (Timestamps): When the record was created and last modified (as stored time keys).
5. Allocation/Weighting: Relative weighting used for reporting/rollups.
6. Project & Structure Links: The project reference plus any linkage to a work-breakdown structure item.
7. Financial Values: Planned budget and/or actual amount assigned/recorded for the CBS item.
