**Cashflow View – Column Categories**
1. **Time Dimension & Periodization**
   Defines the reporting window and aggregation level (yearly/periodic), with both Gregorian and Persian (Solar Hijri) date ranges.
2. **Planned Cashflow Metrics**
   Planned cash inflows, outflows, net cash, and their cumulative values over time.
3. **Actual Cashflow Metrics**
   Realized cash inflows, outflows, net cash, and their cumulative values over time.
4. **Comparative Totals**
   Net planned vs. net actual cash positions, enabling variance and trend analysis.
5. **Project Context**
   Identifies which project the cashflow data belongs to.
