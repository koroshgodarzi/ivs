vw_WBS — Column Categories (concise)

1) WBS Identity & Hierarchy: Unique WBS identifier, parent identifier, level, and hierarchical path information.
2) Work/Activity Definition: Whether item is an activity or milestone, WBS/code fields, name/title, and activity code.
3) Planned Schedule (Dates): Planned start/end dates in both Gregorian and Jalali, plus Jalali Y/M/D components.
4) Actual Schedule (Dates): Actual start/end dates in both Gregorian and Jalali, plus Jalali Y/M/D components.
5) Duration & Units: Duration value and its unit.
6) Progress Metrics: Planned, actual, and field (reported) progress values.
7) Performance/Importance Flags: Weight and critical-path indicator.
8) Project & Ownership Context: Project identifier/name, organization identifier, default plan/WBS identifiers, and whether project has control points.
