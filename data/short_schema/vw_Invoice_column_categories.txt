1. Identity & Contract Link: Invoice identifier plus the contract it belongs to (key + contract id/title).
2. Invoice Reference: Human-facing invoice numbers (system number; optional manual number for balance invoices).
3. Dates (Received & Issued): When the invoice was received/issued.
Note: each date exists as Gregorian + Persian (Solar Hijri) string and as Year/Month/Day parts.
4. Financial Amounts: Invoice amount, previously accepted amount, final accepted amount, and VAT.
5. Cost Breakdown Link (CBS): Optional reference to a Cost Breakdown Structure item (id + name).
6. Balance Flag: Indicates whether the record is a balance/settlement invoice.
